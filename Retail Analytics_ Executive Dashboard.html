Executive summary

What management needs to know.

  • Electronics produced more than half of all sales and more than half of all profit.
  • Dubai sold almost twice as much as Abu Dhabi and more than three times as much as Sharjah.
  • Physical stores are the largest sales channel, but online channels together bring in about 59% of sales.
  • Sales follow the same yearly pattern: strongest in January and February, weakest from September to November.
  • No store-product combination is below its reorder level, yet about one in four combinations has high sales with lower stock.
  • Eight popular products earn less profit than most others, led by Rice (product 1121).

Management takeaways

  • Electronics carries the business. It brings in over half of sales and profit, so any change here matters most.
  • Dubai is the core market. Abu Dhabi and Sharjah are much smaller.
  • Sales are seasonal. Expect strong early-year months and weak autumn months every year.
  • Channels are spread out. Stores lead, with website and app close behind.
  • Watch best sellers with lower stock. No item is below reorder level today, but 2,098 combinations deserve attention.
  • Check margins on eight popular products. They sell well but earn less than most.

Figures marked ≈ are rounded from the charts in the retail analysis. Sales and profit totals are for January 2021 to October 2025. Profit is sales minus product cost.
